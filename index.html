How to use this score app?

Write a score, share it, and rehearse with your team. Each topic takes a minute to learn.

  • Your library
  • Save and return The amber dot on Save means changes are waiting. When you leave, choose Save and leave or Leave without saving.
  • One song, many arrangements Open the version pill beside the title to switch arrangements or add one. Each arrangement keeps its own writing mode and YouTube link.
  • Share and import Use Copy Link on a song row to share it. In My Songs, Attach Link opens a preview before the song loads for editing.
  • Albums
  • Create or join New Album creates a shared album with an invite code. Teammates open Join Album and enter the code with their own account.
  • Roles and access An MD (Music Director) manages songs and people. A Member can play, transpose, change key, meter and BPM, then export or save a private copy. The last MD cannot be removed.
  • Add and copy songs Add from My Songs copies a song with all of its versions into the album. New Song inside an album starts a fresh song there.
  • Writing the score
  • Three writing modes Chord Chart uses chords and rhythm. Numeric Notation uses degrees 1 to 7 with optional lyrics. ChordPro uses lyrics with chords in [brackets].
  • Add or change a chord Click an empty beat and type. Pick a suggestion or press Enter. Click a placed chord to edit it; clear the field and confirm to remove it. Slash chords such as D/F♯ work as typed.
  • ♯ Half-step numbers (naik ½ / turun ½) Type the accidental first: #1 raises the degree and b1 or ♭1 lowers it. The ♮ ♯ ♭ chips filter the suggestions. The score shows a rising slash for a raise and a falling slash for a lower.
  • Chords above the numbers In Chord Chart, turn on Chords On/Off for a section. A number can then sit on the beat with its chord above it, and playback sounds both.
  • ChordPro, step by step Start with Intro ([C] [Am7] [Dm7] [G7] [Cmaj7]) and Verse ([C]Type your lyric here…). Type chords in [brackets] beside the words. Use {sov}/{eoc} for a section header and {c: play softly} for a comment.
  • Rhythm and holds Right-click a beat, or long-press on touch, to split it into ½, ⅓, or ¼. Fermata adds extra held beats while the metronome keeps ticking.
  • Bars, sections, and lyrics Use the section tools to add bars or sections, and click a section name to rename it. Turn on Lyrics for a field under each beat; paste a full line to spread it across the beats.
  • Key, time, and undo Click the title, key, or time signature to edit it inline. The arrows beside the key transpose every chord. Undo and Redo keep the last 50 changes.
  • Paper and playback
  • Export a PDF Export .pdf previews the page before saving. Adjust chord size, lyric size, beat spacing, and bar numbers; each song remembers its own settings.
  • Playback and tempo Play starts from the active section at the song's BPM. The same button becomes Stop, and leaving the editor asks before playback is interrupted.
